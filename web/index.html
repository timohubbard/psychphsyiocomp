Session capture

This records video and audio from your camera and microphone.

Your recording is processed to derive gaze, facial-movement and voice measurements. Video and audio are identifiable data — face blurring alone does not anonymise a recording. You can stop at any time, and nothing is uploaded until you choose to finish.

Lighting is checked because measurement accuracy depends on it, and that dependence is not evenly distributed — camera-based physiological signals degrade more for darker skin tones in dim light, and the gap largely closes under good lighting. Facing a window or lamp rather than sitting with one behind you makes the measurement fairer, not just cleaner.
